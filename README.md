## Bearnie
Bearnie is a simple desktop pet created in 10 hours for the Hack Club "Playground" Event. (ENDED OCTOBER 12, 2026)

# INTRODUCTION
Bearnie works on linux and windows. Mac OS is not a supported OS. to download go to the builds folder and download it. Unzip the folder and you can find the version of bearnie that works for you.

# What does he do?
You can do a decent bit with him, if you left click him you can pet him, if you right click him, you can feed him, make him take a nap, let him dance, or take out toys, theres 4 toys, A ball, a rubber duck, a honey pot, & a Pogo stick.
You can throw the ball at bearnie, its very bouncy, the rubber ducky is slightly less bouncy. Bearnie can jump on the pogo stick and you can feed bearnie the honey.
